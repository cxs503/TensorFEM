# Ice coupon measurements

All input CSVs use SI units. The raw figure PDFs are referenced by DOI or publisher URL. Reproducing the FE comparisons only needs the CSVs.

* `bending.csv` transcribes the raw columns of Table 1 of Ren, Park, Hwang, Jeong and Kim (2017), POAC17-054. Figure 4 specifies a 180 mm support span and 2.5 mm/s punch speed. Photo 1 locates the vertical crack near midspan. Runs 02–04 determine one common modulus and flexural strength. Runs 05 and 10 are held out. Table 1 provides endpoints, so experimental post-peak work is unavailable for these two specimens. [Paper](https://poac.com/Papers/2017/pdf/POAC17_054_Jong-Chun_New.pdf)
* `indentation.csv` records the red trace in Fig. 6 of Liefferink et al. (2021), CC BY 4.0. Read the 183 coloured circular vector markers in drawing order, including unloading, from PDF page 9. In PDF points, the 0 and 20 N ticks are at x = 85.186 and 124.665. The 0 and 0.1 mm ticks are at y = 581.049 and 548.280. Each marker coordinate is its bounding-box centre. The red stroke is RGB (0.9333, 0.2039, 0.1412). The experiment uses a 1.6 mm radius indenter at 3.8 µm/s. The paper gives a colour scale rather than a specimen ID or an exact temperature for each curve. The file SHA-256 for this publisher PDF is `ea89f55498c463ae0d78d1436bcb278d898798cd82fb98b2d1df41eed1bdaa9a`. [Paper and licence](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.11.011025)
* `prydz_curves.csv` digitizes the three force–deflection traces in Fig. 5 of Wang et al. (2022), CC BY 4.0. Points follow each loading and descending branch, ending where its plotted line ends. Coordinates were read on a 740 px-wide version of the plot. Pixel (94,517) is the origin, x=724 is 1 mm and y=9 is 300 N. Reading uncertainty of about 2 pixels corresponds to 0.0032 mm or 1.2 N prior to integration. The figure identifies ice classes. The class curves are not joined to unlabelled specimen numbers. Work is the signed area under each available curve in acquisition order. It includes the response of the specimen and test setup. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.5194/tc-16-1941-2022) · [Companion specimen data](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5787915)
* The 1.5 J/m² cohesive energy is a separate literature input for freshwater ice at −13 °C from Goodman and Tabor's notched bend experiments. It is transferred as an assumption for the Ren specimens. Calibrating a law with that value precludes using it as an independent validation of that law. [Fracture toughness study](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-glaciology/article/fracture-toughness-of-ice-a-preliminary-account-of-some-new-experiments/87E062E1284661430F7346A643015532)

The 10% screening gates for the ice comparisons are engineering choices for this study. Reported experimental uncertainty and within-sample variation remain separate from those choices.
