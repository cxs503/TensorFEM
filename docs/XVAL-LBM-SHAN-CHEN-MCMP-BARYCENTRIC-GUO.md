## ① 问题设定与基准出处

Two-component (multi-component multiphase, MCMP) Shan-Chen LBM is the fluid-side
enabler for gas-layer drag reduction, cavitation inception and gas/liquid mixing in
the ship hydrodynamics platform.  The mature open-source formulation (lbmpy, TCLB)
combines three ingredients that a naive per-component scheme lacks: a **barycentric
mixture velocity**, **Guo second-order forcing** with the `(1 - 1/(2τ))` correction and
an **exponential pseudo-potential**.

Reference (external, executable in-house):
`lssfau/lbmpy` `tests/shan_chen/test_shan_chen_two_component.py`; the reference density
profiles in that test were generated by the independent C++ implementation
`lbm-principles-practice/code/chapter9/shanchen.cpp`
(`nsteps = 1000`, `nfluids = 2`, `gA = 0`).  Case: `{'N': 64, 'omega_a': 1.0, 'omega_b': 1.0, 'g_ab': 6.0, 'rho0': 1.0, 'steps': 1000, 'rho_a_init': [0.1, 0.9], 'rho_b_init': [0.9, 0.1]}`.
The OSS test itself was executed on this machine (lbmpy 2.0 + pystencils 2.0) and passes,
so the reference is live and not a stale text.

## ② 方法与实现

Implementation: `src/tensorfem/lbm_shan_chen_mcmp.py` (NumPy float64, periodic D2Q9).

    f_i^s(x+c_i,t+1) = f_i^s - (f_i^s - f_i^eq,s)/tau_s + (1 - 1/(2 tau_s)) S_i^s
    f_i^eq,s = w_i rho_s [1 + 3 c_i.u_s* + 4.5 (c_i.u_s*)^2 - 1.5 u_s*^2]
    u_s* = u' + (F_s/(2 rho_s)  |  tau_s F_s/rho_s  |  0)      (eq_shift arm)
    u'   = sum_s (m_s + F_s/2)/tau_s / sum_s rho_s/tau_s        (barycentric, mode "tau")
    F_s  = - psi_s(x) sum_s' g_ss' sum_i w_i c_i psi_s'(x+c_i)
    psi(rho) = rho0 (1 - exp(-rho/rho0))

Ablation hooks: `velocity_mode` in {tau, mass, self}, `force_scheme` in {guo, shift},
`eq_shift` in {half, none, tau}, `source_velocity` in {forced, bary}.

## ⑤ 收敛性/敏感性

Two resolutions (64², 96²) and seven couplings `g_ab = [0.1, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0, 3.0, 4.0, 6.0]` are
reported in §4.2/§4.3; the stability boundary is monotone in `g_ab` (the scheme becomes
unstable at progressively earlier steps as the cross coupling grows), which is the
signature of an over-strong equilibrium velocity shift rather than random noise.

## ⑥ 失败模式与工件

* **失败模式**: with `g_ab = 6` the density field becomes non-finite within 30 steps of a
  sharp component step; the maximum density reaches O(10^3–10^4) before blow-up
  (`rho_max_last_finite` in `results.json`).  Root-cause hypothesis (not yet proven): the
  equilibrium velocity shift `F_s/(2 rho_s)` is evaluated with the *dilute* component
  density (`rho ~ 0.05–0.1`) right at the interface, giving `|u*| = O(1)` — i.e. the
  same half-force shift that is harmless at `rho ~ 1` is supersonic for the lean
  component.  The OSS reference subtracts the force half-step from the *previous*
  time level before computing `u'` (two-kernel `u_temp`/`u_corr` dance in the lbmpy
  test), which is an O(F) difference in the lean component and is the most likely
  missing ingredient.  Candidate fixes for round 3: (a) reproduce the two-kernel lagged
  `u'` exactly, (b) EDM (exact difference) force discretisation, (c) density-floor /
  flux limiter on `S_i`.
* **Laplace arm** (`g_ab = 1`) is stable but the two components barely demix
  (rho_tot in/out differ by < 2 %), so the measured `dp` is dominated by the
  finite-domain offset `c` rather than by `sigma/R` — a *withheld* result, not a pass.
