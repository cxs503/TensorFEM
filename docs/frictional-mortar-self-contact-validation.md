# Incremental frictional mortar and self-contact

## Formulation and limits

`frictional_mortar.py` extends the low-order surface-quadrature coupling with
an immutable state at every TRI3/QUAD4 integration point. The state records
tangential elastic slip, accumulated dissipation, previous projection and
master face. A Coulomb elastic-predictor/return-map update supplies stick/slip
traction; slave and master consistent nodal forces are assembled once.

This remains one-pass, penalty, small-sliding mortar. It is not dual-Lagrange,
two-pass unbiased or large-sliding frictional mortar. State updates are pure:
the caller commits the returned state only after a global increment converges.

## Frictional verification

- A unit sliding patch has normal resultant `100` and Coulomb resultant `30`.
  Tangential work equals recoverable spring energy plus dissipation to
  round-off; slave and master resultants cancel.
- Rejecting a sliding trial and retrying from the original state produces the
  analytical sticking force `10`. Committing the sliding state first produces
  the Coulomb-limited force `30`, proving explicit rollback semantics.
- A 3x3 slave mesh sliding on a nonmatching 2x2 master mesh reproduces normal
  force `200` and sticking force `5`, both to round-off. The measured global
  moment equals the physical couple generated by tangential traction acting
  across the `0.001` penalty gap.
- Connectivity changes and invalid penalties fail closed.

All advertised force and energy errors are below 3% (the tests attain
round-off).

## Self-contact increment foundation

The existing topology/AABB candidate pairs are now connected to actual force
updates. Every unordered nonadjacent pair is assembled exactly once. Slave
quadrature points project onto the other facet, and a two-sided clearance law
pushes them apart along the closest-point direction. The same shape-function
weights generate equal and opposite forces.

In the connected four-triangle folded-strip case, `(0,3)` is the sole
candidate and active pair. Maximum penetration is `0.0624600`; force balance
is `7.1e-15` and moment balance is `2.2e-14` in absolute norm.

This qualifies frictionless incremental self-contact force assembly only. It
does not yet provide self-contact Coulomb history, continuous collision
detection, symmetric two-sided quadrature, or a global nonlinear solve. The
frictional history in this module applies to designated slave/master surfaces,
not dynamically changing self-contact pairs.
