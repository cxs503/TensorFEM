# Two-dimensional continuum classic benchmarks

## Distorted Q4 constant-strain patch

A bilinearly mapped, non-parallelogram 4 by 3 mesh is prescribed an affine
displacement field.  The interior displacement error is required to be below
`1e-11`.  This checks isoparametric mapping, Gauss integration, assembly and
Dirichlet elimination independently of any beam-theory approximation.

## Cook membrane

The tapered plane-stress panel has corners `(0,0)`, `(48,44)`, `(48,60)`, and
`(0,44)`, thickness 1, `E=1`, and `nu=1/3`.  Its left edge is fixed and a
uniform vertical traction with unit resultant acts on the right edge.  The
metric is upper-right vertical displacement against the accepted converged
reference `23.96`.

The benchmark is the Cook membrane used in J. C. Simo and M. S. Rifai,
"A class of mixed assumed strain methods and the method of incompatible
modes", *International Journal for Numerical Methods in Engineering* 29
(1990), 1595–1638, DOI: `10.1002/nme.1620290802`.  The numerical reference
and canonical setup are also reproduced in many open implementations; the
test deliberately compares against the published limit rather than a result
generated by TensorFEM.

The 16x16 mesh must have relative error strictly below 3%.  A separate
4/8/16 sequence must decrease monotonically, providing a mesh-convergence
check rather than a single tuned discretisation.
