## 1. Why this case exists

The previous ladder certificate (`docs/CASE-FEM-PLATE-PATCH-PLASTIC-MESH-LADDER.md`) left the three-level mesh convergence and the *limit vs Ilyushin* item open and handed them to queue job `cj3424`. That job has been `running` for **900+ minutes** and never produced an `nx = 16` artifact, but it did leave two `nx = 8 / 12` artifacts behind. Re-extracting them exposed two fail-open holes:

| # | defect | evidence in the archived artifact |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | a **non-equilibrium step is committed to the path** | `nx = 12`: centre deflection `0.0696 m -> 133.49 m` in one increment (a 20 mm plate), equivalent plastic strain `35.4`, section invariant `|M|/m_p = 1.0769` while the 4-layer capacity ratio is exactly `1.0000` |
| D2 | the extractor **reports a limit load fitted through that step** | the two line rule returned `1.825 MPa` (+20.5 %% vs Ilyushin) with a plausible 6.8 %% criteria spread; the three stiffness criteria fired only because the ratios were computed with the runaway slope `1.5e-3` instead of `2.96e-7` |
| D3 | a **stiffening** branch also satisfies the two-line precondition | `k1 > k0` is enough for the rule to fire, and the intersection of two stiffening lines can be negative — nothing rejected it |

Fixes: `path_admissibility()` (section-capacity / monotonicity / finiteness gate), `limit_load_from_path()` now returns `limit_load = None` with a reason on an inadmissible path and rejects measurements outside `(0, peak]`, and `run_patch_plastic_path()` validates each increment **before** committing it (`PatchPath.truncation_reason`).

## 5. Honest reading (what this does and does not establish)

* The FE limit load **moves by 39 %** between nx = 8 (1.1997 MPa) and nx = 12 (1.6695 MPa): it is not a converged number, and the two ramps also differ in density (5 vs 21 load points), so the difference mixes mesh and ramp effects.
* The **Ilyushin double-sine bound is not an admissible upper bound for a patch load**: the FE tangent-criterion load exceeds it by 10.2 % at nx = 12 while staying below the mechanism bound. The module docstring already warned that the sine bound drops the twist term; this is the measurement that confirms it.
* `limit_load` remains a **criterion-based convention** on a curve that does not flatten (final / elastic slope > 1 on both meshes): a layered Mindlin plate under load control redistributes the moment field instead of failing. The honest band is the criteria spread, not the single number.
* Closing the item properly needs **displacement-controlled (arc-length) loading** plus a through-thickness layer-refinement study; that is beyond a load-controlled ladder and is proposed as a follow-up.
* `permanent_set` is reported only for nx = 8 (5.21 mm): the nx = 12 ramp was truncated by the admissibility guard, so no unload leg exists and the value is withheld rather than guessed.
